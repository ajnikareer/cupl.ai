1. The Problem

Planning together is harder than it looks.

🤷

Two people, two tastes

One loves Sufi nights, the other loves Punjabi dhol. Most of the stress in couple planning comes from guessing and compromising, not from the logistics.

📋

Generic advice everywhere

Wedding sites, blogs and apps give everyone the same "Top 10 venues" and "Trending décor". None of it is about you two.

🧩

Scattered decisions

Music, food, outfits, venues and trips get decided in different chats, notes and spreadsheets, and the reasons behind each choice get lost.

💔

The charm fades

After the wedding, life gets busy. Couples forget the little things the other loves: a favourite dish, a song, a dream place. The effort slowly stops.

2. The Idea

"Not just a wedding planner. A companion that knows you both and stays with you after the wedding."

WeaveWed is an AI agent you talk to, by voice or chat, like a warm and friendly wedding planner. It gets to know each partner's taste in music, food, fashion, travel, films, places and brands through natural conversation and quick tap-to-pick choices. Using QLOO's cultural taste intelligence, it finds what you both genuinely love and turns that into practical plans for each event in your journey.

The same agent stays with you after the wedding. It remembers, learns from every trip and celebration, reminds one partner of what the other loves, and helps plan surprises.

🎙 Talk, don't fill forms

The agent is the main way you use the app. It asks, suggests, listens and plans.

💞 Built for two

Each partner has their own account and taste profile. Plans are built from both.

📅 Every chapter

Prewedding → Engagement → Wedding → Honeymoon → Trips → Life Beyond.

🧠 Gets better with time

Every conversation, choice and memory makes the next plan more you.

Step by step

  • Sign up. The agent welcomes you. With no Event Space yet, it acts as a Guide: it explains WeaveWed and helps you get started.
  • Create an Event Space. Tell the agent "We're getting married in Udaipur in February", or use the short wizard. The agent asks for anything missing: date, city, budget, guest count.
  • Invite your partner. They join from their own account, so both voices count.
  • Share tastes. The agent chats with each of you separately and shows live suggestions you can tap.
  • See your Couple Taste DNA. Shared loves, individual favourites, and gentle differences.
  • Get the plan. Organised by sections, each with recommendations and a "Why this?" reason.
  • Refine by talking. "Karan doesn't like loud DJs" and the plan updates. Like, skip or mark items done.

The golden rule

No Event Space = no planning.
Without an active Event Space, the agent stays in Guide mode: it answers questions about the app and offers to create a space. Plans always belong to an event, so every recommendation has the right context: who, where, when and how much.

Web and mobile

The same experience on phone and computer. Talk to the agent on the go, review the full plan on a bigger screen. Everything stays in sync for both partners.

7. Getting to Know Your Tastes

Not a boring questionnaire. A friendly conversation.

Taste areas

  • Music: singers, bands, genres
  • Food: cuisines, favourite dishes, restaurants
  • Films & shows: favourite movies, actors, series
  • Fashion & brands: designers, styles, brands you love
  • Travel & places: dream destinations, beach or mountains, city or nature
  • Books & podcasts: what you read and listen to
  • Experiences: adventure, relaxing, culture, nightlife

How tastes are collected

  • The agent asks one question at a time, naturally
  • Live suggestion chips: tap to pick, search, or add your own
  • Voice works too: just say the names
  • Tastes also come up in normal chat ("I hate crowded beaches") and get saved automatically
  • Each partner shares separately, so it's honest and nobody overrides the other
  • You can view and edit your taste profile any time

8. Couple Taste DNA

The heart of WeaveWed: where your two worlds meet.

💞 Shared Loves

What you both genuinely love. These become the core theme of your plans.

e.g. "You both love Sufi music and coastal food."

🌗 Your Unique Flavours

Things only one of you loves. WeaveWed adds these as personal touches so each of you feels seen.

⚖️ Gentle Differences

Where your tastes differ. The agent suggests a middle ground instead of picking a winner.

e.g. "Beach vs mountains → a hill station with a lake view."

🎨 Your Couple Vibe

A short, shareable description of your style as a couple, like "Soulful, coastal, vintage-romantic", plus a colour and mood palette.

11. Memory & Learning

The longer you use it, the better it knows you.

What it remembers

  • Each partner's tastes, likes and dislikes
  • Your shared loves and differences
  • Decisions you've made ("We chose a palace venue")
  • Your feedback on every recommendation
  • How past events and trips went: what you loved and what you didn't

How it learns

  • From conversations: new tastes mentioned in chat are saved
  • From your choices: likes, skips and "done" teach it what works
  • From experiences: after an event or trip, the agent asks how it went and remembers
  • From changes over time: newer preferences gently replace old ones
Example: On your Goa trip you told the agent you loved the quiet shack dinner but hated the crowded beach. A year later, for your anniversary trip, it suggests quiet, scenic places with great local food and explains that this comes from your Goa experience.

You can always see what the agent remembers about you, edit it, or delete it.

13. Privacy & Surprise Mode

🔒 Your profile, your control

Each partner decides which tastes are shared with the other. Private tastes still shape shared plans but are never shown directly.

🤫 Surprise Mode

Surprise plans and gift ideas are visible only to the person planning them. The partner's agent never brings them up.

🗑 Forget anytime

View, edit or delete any memory. Leaving a couple space removes your data from it.

🙅 No selling data

Your tastes are used only to plan for you.

16. What WeaveWed Does Not Do (Yet)

We keep it honest.

  • It doesn't book venues, vendors or tickets. It recommends and links out so you can book yourself.
  • Prices are estimates to guide budgeting, not quotes.
  • It doesn't replace family conversations. It helps you both arrive with clarity.
  • It doesn't share a partner's private tastes or reveal surprises.
  • No fake reviews, no fake data. Everything comes from your tastes and live intelligence.

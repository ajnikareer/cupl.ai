# SKILL: WEDDING
What matters: city & venue style, dates, guest count, budget, which ceremonies (Roka, Haldi, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding,
Reception, Cocktail), culture/traditions, the couple's vibe. Tastes that matter most: music (Sangeet, entry songs),
food (menu, couple's special dish), fashion (designers, coordinated looks), films (story touches, themes).
Natural order: city → date → guests → budget → ceremonies, interleaved with music/food/fashion tastes.
Tips: ask about family traditions gently; suggest a "couple's special" dish from both food loves; table names from
films they both love; Sangeet playlist mixing both partners' artists plus crowd-pleasers. Typical lead times:
venue 4–6 months, designer outfits ~2.5 months, invitations ~6 weeks.

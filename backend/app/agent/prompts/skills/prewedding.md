# SKILL: PREWEDDING
What matters: shoot city, date window, mood (romantic, candid, royal, vintage, nature), budget; also proposal and date
ideas. Tastes: films (shoot concepts!), music (reel songs), fashion, favourite places.
Tips: build shoot concepts from films they both love (e.g. a retro Bollywood frame); golden-hour heritage spots; outfit
changes that tell a story.
